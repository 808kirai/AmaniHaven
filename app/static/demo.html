Things to try

  1. Text something everyday, like exam stress. You get a self-help tip straight away and no case opens, because most people don't need a counselor.
  2. Text "Nimechoka na maisha". It's unclear, so the service asks directly about suicide. Answer NDIO and watch a crisis case open here, with the on-call counselor paged.
  3. Reply as the counselor from the case card. Your reply arrives on the phone like a normal SMS.
  4. Dial the code to try the menu: breathing exercises, a four-question check-in, or asking for a call.
